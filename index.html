About

Why I do this

Education B.Tech. Computer Engineering
Ladoke Akintola University of Technology, Ogbomosho

I'm a data analyst who enjoys taking messy, disconnected information and turning it into dashboards, reports, and tools that people actually use to make decisions. My foundation is in Computer Engineering, but the bulk of my hands-on experience has come from building real, end-to-end projects with Excel, Power BI, SQL, and Python.

A few examples: I built a clinical trial data analysis on diabetes treatment in Python, handling data cleaning, statistical assessment, and visualization. I used Power BI to build sales and patient-risk dashboards with time-intelligence measures and custom DAX scorecards. And on the Excel side, I've built a series of business tools from scratch — sales, logistics, and inventory trackers, each with a live KPI dashboard and automated formulas — the kind of thing a small business or operations team could plug in and start using immediately.

What sets me apart: I don't stop at analysis — I build things end-to-end, so the output is something a real user can open and act on.

My NYSC service year in the Computer Engineering Department at Lagos State University of Science and Technology involved managing and verifying large volumes of student records, which sharpened my attention to detail and data accuracy. Before that, I worked as a Computer Repair Technician, which taught me structured troubleshooting and how to explain technical issues clearly — a skill that carries directly into presenting data findings to non-technical stakeholders.
